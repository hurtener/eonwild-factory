# Research notes and evidence boundaries

This identifies useful evidence categories. It does not convert published analogs into exact Tarbosaurus truth.

## Volumetric mass properties

- Bates et al. (2009), *Estimating Mass Properties of Dinosaurs Using Laser Imaging and 3D Computer Modelling*. DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0004532`.
- Hutchinson et al. (2011), *A Computational Analysis of Limb and Body Dimensions in Tyrannosaurus rex with Implications for Locomotion, Ontogeny, and Growth*. DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0026037`.

These support inspectable segment-volume, COM, inertia, and sensitivity workflows. T. rex results are tyrannosaurid analog evidence, not Tarbosaurus values.

## Ontogeny

Growth can alter torso and limb proportions, COM, inertia, and feasible locomotion. Uniform scale plus playback speed is insufficient. Published tyrannosaur ontogeny is methodology/analog evidence; the game still needs explicit applicability and uncertainty.

## Turning

- Snively et al. (2019), tyrannosaurid turning and rotational inertia. DOI `10.7717/peerj.6432`.

Turning authority should depend on mass distribution, yaw inertia, force/torque capacity, speed, and footing—not a fixed animation yaw rate.

## Dynamic similarity

- Daley & Birn-Jeffery (2018), avian bipedal scaling. DOI `10.1242/jeb.152538`.

Froude number is useful as a baseline, but living bipeds can deviate from simple dynamic-similarity predictions. It should not be the sole cadence/stride generator.

## Joint limits

- Manafzadeh et al. (2024), articular surface interactions and dinosaurian locomotor poses. DOI `10.1038/s41467-024-44832-z`.

Bone-on-bone limits are not habitual soft-tissue motion envelopes. Store hard evidence limits separately from preferred locomotor limits.

## Tail dynamics

- van Bijlert et al. (2021), tyrannosaur tail natural frequency/walking implications. DOI `10.1098/rsos.201441`.

This supports treating the tail as massive and mechanically coupled, not a ready-made sine curve.

## Centroidal planning and animation

- Orin & Goswami (2008), centroidal momentum matrix. DOI `10.1109/IROS.2008.4650772`.
- Sok et al. (2010), momentum/force-aware motion editing. DOI `10.2312/SCA/SCA10/011-019`.
- Kovar et al. (2002), footskate cleanup. DOI `10.1145/545261.545277`.

These are engineering methods, not biological claims.

## Cranial mechanics

- Johnson-Ransom et al. (2024), tyrannosauroid cranial biomechanics. DOI `10.1002/ar.25326`.

Cranial work can constrain broad head/bite mechanics but does not provide a whole-body attack performance.

## Evidence-status vocabulary

```text
supported
plausible_inference
disputed
speculative
gameplay_only
gameplay_hypothesis
synthetic_test_fixture
```

Source-backed values also record taxon/sample applicability, method, lower/central/upper estimate, game transformation, reviewer, and review date.
