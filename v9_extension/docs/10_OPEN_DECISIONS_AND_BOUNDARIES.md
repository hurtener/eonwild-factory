# Open decisions, assumptions, and explicit non-decisions

## Decisions already made by this handoff

- V8.2 is the approved relaxed-walk authority.
- V5.5 is mined for capabilities and intent, not copied wholesale.
- V8.3 has separate compatibility and balanced walk outputs.
- Legacy V8.3 balance is labeled a support-phase/posture proxy.
- Root-motion and in-place clips come from one world plan.
- Generic code uses semantic roles and family/species profiles.
- Generic roar is split into ecologically meaningful call semantics.
- V9 uses authored MotionPrograms plus reduced-order biomechanics, not physics-as-director.
- The ballistic path applies to whole-body COM.
- Passive mass properties and active capacity are separate contracts.
- Growth tiers use structural mass/proportions with hysteresis and continuous parameters.

## Open engineering decisions

These must be resolved through evidence, not silently guessed:

1. Exact V8.3 balanced-walk pelvis/contact tolerances.
2. Whether the V8.2 source rig needs any foot-pivot migration; default is no.
3. Which V5.5 terrain/turn features are P1 in the first V8.3 release versus a V8.3.1 follow-up.
4. Final canonical implementation language boundaries between offline Python/Blender and runtime TypeScript/Babylon.
5. Exact event embedding strategy: GLB extras, sidecar manifest, or both.
6. Final resting posture interpretation for lie/rest/rise.
7. Tarbosaurus joint hard/preferred envelopes and actuation capacities.
8. Reviewed segment mass distributions and uncertainty ranges.
9. Whether a heavy adult can perform the airborne power attack at all; grounded lunge is mandatory fallback.
10. Second real species fixture used after the synthetic test body.

## Scientific non-decisions

The current materials do not establish exact Tarbosaurus:

- body mass for the individual fixture;
- segment mass fractions;
- muscle force/torque/power;
- joint soft-tissue limits;
- maximum speed or jump authority;
- landing tolerance;
- habitual resting posture;
- vocalization;
- airborne attack behavior.

Profiles may contain normalized or provisional values for engineering tests only. They must remain labeled and cannot become scientific or marketing claims.

## Scope non-decisions

This handoff does not require:

- full muscle simulation;
- real-time whole-body nonlinear optimization in the browser;
- pure ragdoll locomotion;
- all dinosaur families at once;
- exact physical interaction with every carcass material;
- final game balance numbers;
- an airborne attack if feasibility gates reject it.
