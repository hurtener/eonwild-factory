## What Eonwild is

Eonwild is a browser-first, solo-complete ecological dinosaur survival game. Each life is a compressed meaningful season in a continuous prehistoric world. The player inhabits a scientifically grounded—but explicitly game-interpreted—animal, reads water, food, weather, herds, predators, terrain, calls, tracks, and body condition, then chooses whether to roam, migrate, hunt, evade, cooperate, rest, or take a risk. Meaningful animal behavior produces Lineage, unlocking species as distinct ecological roles rather than power tiers.

## Invariants relevant to this handoff

- [ ] Convincing animal embodiment remains the first quality impression.
- [ ] Ecology creates pressure rather than arbitrary quests.
- [ ] The world remains continuous, roamable, looped, and non-corridor.
- [ ] Species remain roles/sidegrades rather than a power ladder.
- [ ] Solo remains complete.
- [ ] A life respects player time and produces meaningful failure.
- [ ] Scientific evidence, uncertainty, and gameplay interpretation remain separate.
- [ ] No protected reference content entered production.
- [ ] No paid power, crafting drift, runtime LLM dependency, or hidden manual production step was introduced.
