# V5.5 balance correction — handoff summary

## Outcome

V5.5 is an independent full-solver iteration lane. The V5 directory remains byte-for-byte unchanged.

The release contains 37 clips, including 16 endpoint-exact transitions, with the original 75-joint skin order preserved. The accepted lower-foot calibration changes only the two corresponding inverse-bind entries while preserving the undeformed skinned mesh. The generated GLB SHA-256 is `4e6e85dc82e941a3fc10109ca781dd3dcbb084699dc19834bf8683c04b22fe65`.

## Implemented corrections

- Neutral and relaxed idle use an asymmetric support arrangement: one leg carries the body while the other foot rests forward.
- Pelvis translations generated by locomotion and actions are now exported, including in-place clips.
- Relaxed walk shortens maximum rear ankle reach by 10.9%, starts recovery from the hip, and delays knee/ankle/toe response through the swing chain.
- The accepted Test 2 lower-foot placement is encoded as a `0.90` foot-to-toe-gap fraction in the versioned Tarbosaurus V5.5 profile. The builder measures and applies it independently to both legs; no fixed coordinates or animation frames are hardcoded.
- Power bite delays visible jaw preparation until after rear/down loading, drops the pelvis 8.2% of hip height, then drives 20% of hip height forward through fixed-foot leg extension.
- Eating uses a slow loop-safe pelvis settle with 4.95% hip-height vertical excursion, fixed feet, bounded torso pitch, and tail counterbalance.
- Existing V5 jaw calibration, expressive-track polish, and exact transition construction remain in the V5.5 build.

## Rejected candidates

- Candidate 1 did not make the support stagger sufficiently legible.
- Candidate 2 increased solver sampling/continuity but worsened acceleration gates.
- Candidate 3 passed numerical gates but was perceptually rejected because attack and eating still read as head-led.
- Candidate 4 corrected the body action but exposed an inherited V5 jaw-layer overwrite that still opened the mouth too early.
- Candidate 5 delays the calibrated jaw track until after the body preload and is the release candidate.

## Known limitations

- The walk reference supports a repeatable phase hypothesis, not laboratory-grade kinetics.
- The attack/eat camera changes, so those observations are qualitative.
- GPU acceleration was not added; the solver remains deterministic CPU work while Blender rendering uses Metal.
