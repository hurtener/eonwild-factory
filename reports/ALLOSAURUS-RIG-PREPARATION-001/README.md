## Reproducible operation

`factory.rig_preparation` admits one frozen GLB and one strict declarative
configuration. It validates the selected skin, hierarchy, explicit
right-handed source-world axes, declared pivot coordinates, and articulation
branch. It then reparents or relocates declared joints, rebuilds inverse-bind
matrices for every skin joint, reopens the encoded GLB, and rejects excessive
world-matrix or neutral-skin reconstruction error. It first rejects a source
whose full-weight neutral skin is not canonical POSITION geometry and rejects
multi-skin sources, so rewriting one inverse-bind accessor cannot silently
alter another skin. Mesh primitives, position and texture buffers, material
data, and the visible neutral surface are retained. A declared upper-skull
ownership repair changes JOINTS/WEIGHTS only within its source-world selector.

The Allosaurus configuration:

- declares source world (+X) lateral, (+Y) up, and (+Z) forward;
- reparents the bilateral proximal leg joints `Bone_023` and `Bone_029`
  from anterior axial `Bone_002` to measured pelvis `Bone_001`, while
  preserving their world origins;
- relocates the knee and ankle origins on both sides along the measured source
  chain;
- declares `Bone_044`, directly below head `Bone_045`, as a lower-jaw
  articulation with descendants `Bone_043` and `Bone_042`, about a fixed
  parent-local axis;
- transfers existing jaw-branch influence to `Bone_045` for a declared
  6,672-vertex upper-skull halfspace. The selector uses source geometry and
  explicit axes, independent of the source's incorrect jaw weights.

## Hindlimb evidence

The source's provisionally interpreted first four leg joints have normalized
segment lengths `0.32570 / 0.44320 / 0.23110`, with femur-to-tibia ratio
`0.73487`. [White et al. (2013), PLOS ONE 8:e68649, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3722220/)
reports
Allosaurus fragilis UUVP 6000r femur/tibia/metatarsus lengths of
`880 / 730 / 375 mm`, or `0.4433 / 0.3677 / 0.1890`. Those measurements
are used only as a specimen-specific proportion sanity check; the artist model
is not established as UUVP 6000r and the measurements do not locate exact
surface landmarks.

The prepared source measures `0.441374 / 0.367826 / 0.190800` on each side,
with femur-to-tibia ratio `1.199951`. Bilateral joint-chain reflection differs
by at most `7.4718516340306e-08 m`. These are source-world joint-center
measurements, not biological calibration or proof of anatomical correctness.
The original artist rig deliberately used straight, parallel legs to avoid
positional drift; its articulation origins and inferred segment lengths are
placeholders. The relocated knee and ankle origins merely create a reproducible
provisional ratio-sanity candidate. They are not corrected anatomy, and the
straight neutral pose is not a walking target. A later onboarding stage must
locate hip, knee, ankle, and metatarsophalangeal centers against mesh morphology
and primary anatomical evidence before admitting a natural gait stance.

[Dececchi et al. (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7220109/)
reports a composite USNM 4734/UUVP 6000 Allosaurus row
of 1,512 kg and 1.985 m hindlimb length, with a study hip proxy of 1.588 m.
That coherent row may support a future explicitly declared benchmark after
the corrected source is measured. This stage chooses no mass or scale and does
not force the source hip to the study proxy.

## Jaw and deformation probes

The first `0fe715f` candidate exposed a real weight-ownership defect: the
upper snout moved with the jaw. In the geometry-defined 6,672-vertex upper-skull
halfspace, 5,732 vertices moved more than 1 mm at either (+8) or (-8)
degrees, with median `5.470 mm` and maximum `33.187 mm` displacement. This
supersedes the earlier narrow 588-vertex cranium check.

The corrected `c56772d` candidate transfers jaw-branch weight from 6,132
affected vertices in that halfspace to head `Bone_045`. The entire declared
upper region then has zero jaw-branch weight and zero displacement in both
isolated probes. A geometry-defined lower-side control retains 1,387 vertices,
median `33.799 mm` and maximum `47.140 mm` displacement. The 1,357-vertex
mouth-boundary band contains fixed and moving surfaces: 476 vertices move more
than 1 mm, while its median displacement is zero. Neutral and (+8/-8) head
closeups were inspected; the upper snout remains fixed and the lower jaw opens
and closes. Root's independent conservative dorsal-rostrum box contains 2,151
vertices, all of which moved more than 1 mm before the repair and exactly zero
after it; see `evidence/root-upper-snout-after.json`.

The closeups still show angular/stretching inner-cheek and mouth-corner surface
near the rear lower-jaw tooth region, especially at (+8) degrees. The
upper-snout leakage P1 is closed, while whole-head soft-tissue deformation and
mouth-corner ownership remain **PENDING** polish rather than visual approval.

The ownership halfspace and hinge are inferred engineering landmarks supported
by source geometry, hierarchy, and weights. They are not fossil-derived joint
centers, full anatomical segmentation, or tooth-contact evidence.

Transient bilateral hip/knee/ankle bends remain finite. Their one-percentile
owned-triangle area ratios range from `0.807` to `0.938`; one right-hip
triangle reaches a `0.306` ratio under the isolated 10-degree probe. Side and
front probe renders show no obvious rupture, but these area statistics and
visuals are not collision tests.
