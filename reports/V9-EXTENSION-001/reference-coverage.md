# V9 pose/reference coverage audit

Status: **Phase-0 documentation audit; no V9 artifact approval**

This report covers the 29 Tarbosaurus animation contracts in the V9 extension
pack and the 16 authored V8.3 bridges recovered from V5.5. The classes are:

- **A** — existing engine/mechanical/contact evidence is sufficient; no new user reference is required.
- **B** — a still-image posture target is needed; timing can be authored by the contract and solver.
- **C** — a native-speed video is needed for timing, momentum, force-chain, or contact order.
- **D** — both a posture still and native-speed video are needed.
- **E** — do not visually imitate an assumed animal performance; use a provenance-labeled bounded gameplay/engineering reconstruction.

The requests below are for original project-owned or properly commissioned
reference captures only. No protected external asset source is requested.

## Executive result

Existing evidence is sufficient for the approved relaxed walk, neutral/alert
idle intent, walk start/stop, and the basic endpoint/bridge mechanics inherited
from V5.5. The V8.2 package is authoritative for the relaxed walk. V5.5 attack
and feeding evidence remains qualitative and its balance values are visual
proxies, not physical COM or biological validation. The productized worktree
contains a V8.3 working diagnostic, but its visual approval and promotion are
pending and its blocker report says it is not release evidence.

The prior five native-speed capture packages and one small design-posture still
sheet remain the requested coverage plan. Ten user-supplied MP4s have now been
received externally and are recorded as exact-byte, filename-mapped technical
evidence in `reports/V9-EVIDENCE-INGEST-001/audit.json`. They are not stored in
the productized worktree: the ten duplicate copies were removed pending
confirmation of project ownership or reuse rights. All ten primary video
streams are 24 fps rather than the requested 60 fps, so receipt does not close
any native-speed timing gate or B/C/D requirement. Fixed-camera pairing,
framing, phase-boundary, and choreography acceptance were not adjudicated in
the ingest pass. The airborne power-attack branch, drinking, swallowing,
resting, vocalization, and terminal-death interpretations remain
reconstruction-only and should not receive imitative reference footage.

## Requested user capture packages

All video requests mean native-speed playback, fixed camera, neutral lighting,
no camera tracking/cuts, and no motion blur that hides contact. Keep the entire
animal in frame: head, tail, pelvis, both feet, and the floor. Side and 3/4
takes should use the same scale, lens, floor, and target placement. Record the
actual encoded frame rate and do not retime footage to make a phase fit.

### Must-have

#### `R-GAIT` — distinct faster gait regimes and run transitions

One capture session, paired fixed side and 3/4 takes, **60 fps**, full body,
feet, tail, and head. Produce the contract-length takes below; a few handle
frames before and after are useful but are not part of the contract phase.

| Take | Contract duration | Exact phase/action | Extra camera need |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `fast_walk_loop` | 3.15 s | Two or more steady faster-walk cycles; preserve alternating contacts and a visibly distinct regime from relaxed walk/run | Side + 3/4; front optional |
| `run_loop` | 2.15 s | Two or more steady running cycles; show compression and contact order | Side + 3/4; front useful |
| `sprint_loop` | 1.55 s | Steady sprint cycle including the brief unsupported/compressed interval | Side + 3/4; front useful |
| `run_start` | 3.8 s | Prepare → crouched load → first propulsion/contact → second propulsion → run handoff | Side + 3/4; front useful for lateral catch |
| `run_stop` | 4.5 s | Incoming run → first braking contact/absorption → second braking step → final settle | Side + front/3/4; rear optional |

Why: V5.5/V8.2 prove relaxed walking and walking transitions, but no clean
native run/sprint regime or run-specific acceleration/braking sequence.

#### `R-LISTEN` — alert/listen timing

**1.6 s contract, 60 fps**, fixed side plus front/3/4, full body and feet.
Show interrupt-current-behavior → head/acoustic orientation → perceptible
processing freeze → lower-body preparation → alert handoff. Use a neutral
off-camera cue, not an exaggerated cinematic head snap.

Why: existing looking/alert material supplies intent but not the normal-speed
freeze and delayed body catch required by contract 12.

#### `R-BITE` — quick, committed, low, and miss branches

Fixed side and 3/4, **60 fps**, full body, all feet, head, tail, ground plane,
and a visible target/contact marker. Provide these separate takes:

| Take | Contract duration | Exact phase/action | Still companions |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `quick_bite_hit` | 0.9 s | Acquire → anticipation retract → jaw open → strike/contact → recoil/recovery | Pre-strike and contact stills optional |
| `committed_grounded_bite` | 2.1 s | Target commitment → whole-body alignment/load → hindlimb propulsion → maximum gape before contact → follow-through → long recovery | Required: preload, maximum-gape/contact, recovered posture; side + 3/4 + front |
| `quick_bite_miss_recovery` | 1.6 s | Missing resistance → safe overextension → imbalance → emergency step → neck/body recovery → posture reset | One still at maximum safe overextension useful |
| `low_downward_bite` | 2.2 s | Low-target acquisition → stabilized support → shared lowering → downward contact → absorb/hold → raise/rebalance | Required: stable low support and lowest safe head/neck; side + 3/4 + front |

Why: V5.5 has a longer bite clip and V8.1-era footage shows a broad attack
sequence, but neither supplies the V9 short-bite timing, explicit miss branch,
or grounded whole-body event order. The airborne branch in `power-attack.yaml`
is not a request for imitation footage; it remains feasibility-gated with a
mandatory grounded fallback.

#### `R-FEED` — head-down feeding and tear/pull

Fixed side and 3/4, **60 fps**, full body with both feet, head, tail, floor,
and carcass/contact region visible. Keep the carcass target stationary and
mark the intended contact region.

| Take | Contract duration | Exact phase/action | Extra camera need |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `head_down_feeding` | 8.0 s | Carcass assessment → feeding stance → progressive pelvis/thorax/neck lowering → mouth acquisition → feeding bite → head manipulation/process → target reset/loop | Side + 3/4; top optional for target relation |
| `tear_pull` | 2.8 s | Acquire tissue → rear support → jaw preload/attachment → whole-body pull → tension peak → release/hold → recovery | Side + 3/4; front useful for support symmetry |

Still companions: stable feeding stance, lowest safe head/neck/foot support,
and post-pull recovery. Existing V5.5 eat-loop and attack/eat footage supplies
intent but is not clean enough to validate V9 descent, target contact, pull,
and release timing.

#### `R-SHOVE-STUMBLE` — body pressure and recovery failure

Separate fixed-camera takes at **60 fps**, full body, feet, head, tail, floor,
and target:

| Take | Contract duration | Exact phase/action | Cameras |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `body_shove` | 2.1 s | Contact-region alignment → stance → load → body drive → contact peak/maintain pressure → recovery step | Side + 3/4; front useful |
| `stumble` | 2.1 s | Normal incoming motion → support failure → pelvis drop → tail emergency correction → reachable recovery foot → body catch → head/neck rebound | Side + front/3/4; rear optional |

Why: no existing clip proves target-owned displacement, pressure support, or a
non-resetting stumble with a reachable catch foot.

### Useful, narrowly scoped stills

#### `R-POSES` — design posture sheet

Original high-resolution stills, no fps requirement, fixed neutral floor and
matching scale. These are **gameplay/design targets, not paleobiological
evidence**.

- **Investigate/scent:** side, 3/4, and front; optionally top to show a ground
  evidence point. Pose must show planted feet, restrained neck/thorax lowering,
  and directional attention. Do not depict or request a mammalian/dog sniff
  cycle. This is useful for contract 11 and can share the low-feeding setup.
- **Wounded idle:** left- and right-injury variants from side, 3/4, and front;
  full feet/tail/head. Show healthy-side support bias and guarded pelvis/spine
  posture. This is needed for contract 25 but remains a gameplay target.

No new idle, relaxed-walk, walk-start, or walk-stop reference is requested.

## Reconstruction-only boundary

Do not request visual imitation footage for swallow, drinking, lie-down, rest,
rise, threat/contact calls, death, or the airborne power-attack branch. The
research notes explicitly leave habitual rest posture, vocalization, maximum
speed/jump authority, landing tolerance, exact forces/torques, and airborne
attack behavior unresolved. These states still require authored clips, but the
clips must be labeled `gameplay_only`, `gameplay_hypothesis`,
`plausible_inference`, or `synthetic_test_fixture` as appropriate and must be
bounded by contacts, joint limits, capacity, target relations, and fail-closed
feasibility checks.

## Provenance requirements for requested captures

Each new still/video record must retain creator or generating tool and version,
date, prompt/seed when generative, all input assets and hashes, license and
commercial-use status, human modifications, reviewer, and AI-content
disclosure. The source hierarchy is project-original/commissioned material,
public-domain or commercially compatible material, and independently cited
primary research. Fantastic Dinosaurs may be used for discovery/inspiration
only; its text, imagery, models, animation, sounds, and presentation must not
be copied, scraped, or used as generation input.

For scientific claims, prefer the pack's cited peer-reviewed sources (Bates et
al. 2009, DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0004532`; Hutchinson et al. 2011, DOI
`10.1371/journal.pone.0026037`; Snively et al. 2019, DOI
`10.7717/peerj.6432`; Daley and Birn-Jeffery 2018, DOI
`10.1242/jeb.152538`; Manafzadeh et al. 2024, DOI
`10.1038/s41467-024-44832-z`; van Bijlert et al. 2021, DOI
`10.1098/rsos.201441`; and Johnson-Ransom et al. 2024, DOI
`10.1002/ar.25326`) with taxon applicability and uncertainty retained.
These sources support methods or analog context; they do not license copied
media or establish exact Tarbosaurus performance.

This report records repository paths and observed roles only. It does not claim
that any legacy visual reference establishes Tarbosaurus kinetics, mass,
forces, habitual posture, or vocal behavior.
