# Blockers and limitations

There is no blocker to the requested first executable vertical slice.

The following are intentionally deferred rather than represented as complete:

- hip-balance implementation;
- V5/V5.5 behavior migration;
- browser/editorial HTML and video comparison output;
- a reduced browser-delivery GLB;
- promotion of the real committed V8.2 release.

The family contract is explicitly provisional because this slice validates one
morphology only. It must not be treated as a generally accepted locomotion
grammar until a second meaningfully different morphology passes it.
